About this book

Every physics engine is a stack of numerical compromises, and most of its bugs live in the compromises nobody chose deliberately. This book works through the mathematics under a modern robotics solver — integrators, constraint formulations, contact — with the failure modes given equal billing with the methods.

The final part is unusual for a numerics text: a field guide to debugging a physics engine, organised by symptom. Energy drift, penetration, jitter — what each one looks like, which compromise usually caused it, and how to confirm the diagnosis before touching the code.
