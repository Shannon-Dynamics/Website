The gap on the octree side is pruning
60,288 leaves standing for 76,920 voxels. One merged node stands for eight base voxels — or eight thousand. A leaf is not a voxel.
With free-space carving switched back on the two maps diverge on purpose: a ray passing through erases a voxel one viewpoint saw as occupied while others prove the space empty. Only the octree carves.
